Solar panels convert sunlight into electrical energy. A panel rated at 200 watts produces 200 watt-hours during one hour at its rated output. Four such panels produce a total of 800 watts under those conditions. If the array operates at that output for five hours, it generates 4 kilowatt-hours of electrical energy. Real output varies with cloud cover, panel temperature, shading, and orientation. A battery stores electrical energy for later use, but losses during charging and discharging reduce the energy ultimately delivered to appliances. A simple system with 90 percent round-trip battery efficiency delivers 3.6 kilowatt-hours after storing 4 kilowatt-hours. Power is measured in watts, whereas energy is measured in watt-hours or joules.
